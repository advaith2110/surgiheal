Computer Vision Deep-Dive

Automated SSI Surveillance & Image Biomarkers

Extracting quantifiable surgical indicators from standard smartphone photography.

Erythema Spread Scoring (0-10)
Quantifies redness halos around the incision line. Accurately distinguishes normal reactive healing erythema (<5mm) from expanding microbial cellulitis (>2cm).
Exudate Spectral Characterization
Differentiates benign clear/straw-colored serous fluid from purulent (cloudy yellow/green) exudate—the cardinal visual biomarker of bacterial infection.
Wound Edge Approximation
Audits surgical staple and suture spacing, detecting mechanical tension, minor gap separation, and critical surgical dehiscence (open wound breakdown).
Predictive SSI Risk Percentage
Synthesizes visual markers, post-op day timeline, patient body temperature, and reported pain into a predictive infection probability index.

Emergency Clinical Triage

Catching Silent Killers: DVT, Sepsis & Dehiscence

Active 24/7 symptom screening separating routine healing sensations from life-threatening crises.

🚨 TIER 1: Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT) / Pulmonary Embolism
Triggers: Unilateral calf swelling, cramp-like tenderness, warmth, dyspnea, or chest pain.
Clinical Action: IMMEDIATE Emergency 911 / ER alert. Instructs patient NOT to massage calf (preventing clot embolization). Triggers urgent Doppler ultrasound.
⚠️ TIER 2: Surgical Site Infection (SSI) & Early Sepsis
Triggers: Expanding erythema >2.5cm, purulent exudate, foul odor, or fever >100.4°F (38.0°C).
Clinical Action: Urgent same-day surgical team callback. Guides sterile dry dressing coverage, avoids home ointments, and initiates oral antibiotics.
🟢 TIER 3: Routine Expected Post-Operative Sensations
Triggers: Gentle pulling around sutures, mild itching, light serous fluid, mild bruising.
Clinical Action: Immediate empathetic reassurance, pain pacing guidance, and ice/elevation reminder. Eliminates midnight ER overcrowding.
Human-AI Design Innovation

The Dual-Perspective Switcher

Simultaneously serving patient psychological needs and clinician regulatory documentation.

👤 For Patients: Calm Reassurance Card
  • Empathetic Literacy: Written strictly at a 6th-grade reading level to maximize comprehension and reduce panic.
  • Normalizing Sensation: Explains that mild staple itchiness and light pink borders are expected biological repair signals.
  • Actionable Dos & Don'ts: Step-by-step bullet points on dressing protection, icing intervals, and elevation posture.
  • Explicit Boundary Warnings: Exact clinical triggers specifying when to call the clinic vs when to head to the ER.
🩺 For Clinicians: Structured Technical Audit
  • Quantitative Biomarkers: Exact erythema spread radius (cm), swelling grade, and exudate spectral classification.
  • Wound Approximation Index: Quantitative coaptation audit of surgical staples/sutures to identify partial dehiscence.
  • Standardized CDC Alignment: Formatted directly to CDC NHSN superficial and deep incisional SSI criteria.
  • EHR-Ready Documentation: Structured clinical JSON ready for 1-click export into Epic, Cerner, or Meditech.
